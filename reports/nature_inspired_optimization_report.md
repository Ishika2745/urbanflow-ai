## 2. Problem Formulation & Objective Architecture

### Part C: Equity-Aware Multi-Objective Fitness Function
The multi-objective evaluator balances three normalized criteria:

$$\max F = \alpha \cdot C + \beta \cdot E + \gamma \cdot R - \text{Penalty} - (w_{\text{cost}} \cdot \text{NormCost})$$

Where:
- **$C$ (Normalized Population-Weighted Coverage Improvement):** Marginal population brought within walking catchment ($d_0 = 800\text{m}$) relative to baseline unserved residents.
- **$E$ (Normalized Accessibility Equity Improvement):** Percentage reduction in the accessibility Gini inequality coefficient across all municipal wards.
- **$R$ (Normalized Reduction in Transport Inequality):** Dynamic Transit Inequality Index (DTII) alleviation, directing stops toward verified transit desert corridors (e.g. Mankhurd, Kurla, Malad East).
- **Hard Constraints:** Hard exclusion filter $\ge 350\text{m}$ from existing transit stops, plus a mutual candidate separation penalty for stops closer than $300\text{m}$.

**Active Weights Configuration:**
- $\alpha$ (Coverage): `0.40`
- $\beta$ (Gini Equity): `0.30`
- $\gamma$ (DTII Alleviation): `0.30`
- Budget: `K = 15` stops

## 3. Algorithmic Mechanics

### Algorithm 1: Genetic Algorithm (Equity-GA)
- **Chromosome Representation:** Integer list of $K=15$ unique candidate stop indices.
- **Feasible Initialization:** Samples candidate stops iteratively, rejecting spatial proximity conflicts.
- **Parent Selection:** 3-way Tournament Selection.
- **Set-Preserving Crossover:** Combines parental candidate pools and extracts $K$ non-conflicting stops.
- **Spatial Mutation:** Gene swap operator targeting unselected candidate locations with spatial repair.
- **Elitism:** Retains top 2 individuals across generations.
- **Termination:** Max generations, runtime limits, or convergence stagnation.

### Algorithm 2: Discrete Particle Swarm Optimization (RK-DPSO)
- **Rank-Key Mapping:** Continuous particle position vector $X_i \in \mathbb{R}^M$ represents selection priorities across all $M$ candidates.
- **Decoding & Spatial Constraint Handling:** Sorts candidate priorities in descending order, greedily admitting candidates that satisfy the minimum separation rule ($300\text{m}$) until exactly $K$ stops are selected.
- **Inertia Weight Damping:** Dynamic linear damping ($w: 0.90 \rightarrow 0.40$).
- **Velocity Clamping:** $V_{i, j} \in [-4.0, 4.0]$ preventing saturation.
