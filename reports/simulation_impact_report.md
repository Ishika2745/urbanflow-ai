## 5. Documented Modeling Assumptions (Part E)

1. **Walking Catchment Radius ($d_0$):** Set to **800 meters**, representing approximately an 8-to-10 minute pedestrian walk.
2. **Pedestrian Walking Speed:** Standard urban pedestrian speed of **1.2 m/s (~4.32 km/h)**.
3. **Non-Overlapping Population Guarantee:** Incremental coverage counts strictly citizens beyond 800m of existing stops; commuters with shortened walk are reported separately to prevent double-counting.
4. **Feeder Impact Function:** Each newly placed feeder stop within walking catchment provides a local accessibility boost up to a saturating ceiling of 25.0 points.
5. **Economic Valuation:** Based on average Mumbai urban wage of INR 120/hr, with commuter time saved valued at 50% (INR 60/hr) over 300 annual working days.
